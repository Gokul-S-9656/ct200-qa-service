# CardioTrack CT-200 Home Blood Pressure Monitor

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Purpose
The CT-200 is intended for home blood pressure monitoring by adults.
### 1.2 Intended Users
Patients and caregivers may use the device after reading this manual.

## 2. Device Overview
### 2.1 Components
Main unit, cuff, display, batteries, USB cable.
### 2.2 Display Indicators
SYS, DIA, Pulse, Battery, Error codes.

## 3. Measurement Procedure
### 3.1 Preparation
Sit comfortably for five minutes before measurement.
### 3.2 Taking a Reading
Wrap the cuff correctly and press Start.
### 3.3 Invalid Conditions
Movement, talking, or incorrect cuff placement may invalidate readings.

## 4. Safety
### 4.1 Warnings
Do not use on injured arms.
### 4.2 Contraindications
Not intended for neonatal patients.

## 5. Error Codes
### 5.1 E1
Low battery.
### 5.2 E2
Improper cuff placement.
### 5.3 E3
Overpressure detected. Device shall stop inflation automatically.

## 6. Data Storage
Stores up to 120 readings with timestamp.

## 7. Maintenance
Clean with a soft damp cloth. Do not immerse in water.

## 8. Troubleshooting
Replace batteries if device does not power on.

## 9. Specifications
Pressure range: 0–300 mmHg.
Operating temperature: 10–40°C.

## 10. Disposal
Dispose according to local electronic waste regulations.
